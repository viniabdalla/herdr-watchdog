## Expectations

And this is the underlying reasoning behind making this Herdr plugin:

1. More often than I would like, things get lost, and it's difficult to keep track of things to do in a vibe-coding project. There are things the LLM will take care of, and there are things I have to take care of, and many times they get lost inside the terminal.

2. There are also dependencies between tasks; sometimes one blocks another. The LLM can take care of its own restrictions, but I have to be aware of the things I need to take care of, like testing rounds, passwords, logins, whatever. These things should be kept to a minimum, but they do exist.

3. We will use Herdr's window-splitting capabilities to build a monitoring layout, or maybe even two layouts: one for monitoring project todos and issues, and one to handle sessions and sit next to them, or even two sessions at a time.

4. I will use the LLMs to review this and think of improvements with me.

5. This is kind of a side quest, but it is an important one: I feel things get stuck more often than not. Working in a terminal is great and feels good, but it can sometimes get bloated and overwhelming when managing two different tasks.

6. If possible, I'd like to test the plugin in this project too.

7. When this is done, a framework will be put together to produce more utilities and features for it. I don't want it to get bloated, but we might need to add a few things in the future, and standing still is never healthy.
